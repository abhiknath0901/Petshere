Emergency First Aid Guides

Stop Bleeding

Apply firm pressure with a clean cloth and seek immediate vet care.

Help a Choking Pet

Remove visible objects and perform chest thrusts gently.

Seizure Response

Clear area around your pet, do not restrain, monitor duration.

CPR Guide

Alternate rescue breaths with chest compressions (100–120/min).
