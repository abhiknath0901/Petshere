Shipping Policy

PetSphere – Fast, Safe and Reliable Delivery

1. Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or public holidays.

2. Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates

Shipping charges will be calculated and displayed at checkout. Delivery times may vary depending on your location.

  • Standard Shipping: 3–7 business days
  • Express Shipping: 1–3 business days
  • Local Delivery (if available): Same-day or next-day delivery

3. Shipment Confirmation & Tracking

You will receive a shipment confirmation email with tracking details once your order has been shipped.

4. Delivery Delays

Occasionally, delivery delays may occur due to bad weather, or unforeseen circumstances.

5. Damaged or Lost Packages

PetSphere is not responsible for lost or damaged packages after they have been handed over to the courier. However, we will help you contact the courier for support.

6. International Shipping

International shipping availability and fees vary by location. Customs fees and import charges are the customer's responsibility.

7. Contact Us

For questions about your shipment, email us at support@petsphere.com.
