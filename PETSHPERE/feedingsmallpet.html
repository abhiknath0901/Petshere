Feeding Your Small Pets

Feeding your small pets isn’t just about filling their bowls—it’s about keeping them happy, healthy, and full of energy. Each little pet has its own favorite foods and special needs, so understanding what they enjoy (and what they should avoid) makes a big difference.

1. Rabbits

Rabbits are big fans of hay—and honestly, it should make up most of their diet. Fresh hay helps keep their digestion healthy and their teeth in good shape. They also enjoy leafy greens like spinach and lettuce, along with small amounts of carrots as a treat. Pellets can be given, but not too much. Always keep fresh water available, and avoid sugary or processed foods—they’re not good for your bunny.

👉 Always keep fresh water available, and avoid sugary or processed foods—they’re not good for your bunny.

2. Hamsters

Hamsters may be tiny, but they love a variety of foods. A good quality hamster mix or pellets should be their main meal, but they also enjoy fresh treats like small pieces of carrot, cucumber, or apple (just make sure to remove the seeds). You can occasionally give them a little protein, like a tiny bit of boiled egg or mealworms. But be careful—too many treats or sugary foods can make them sick.

👉 A good tip: give only small portions and clean out any leftover food to keep their space fresh.

3. Guinea Pigs

Guinea pigs are adorable and a bit special when it comes to food—they need Vitamin C every day because their bodies can’t make it on their own. Along with hay and pellets, you can give them fresh veggies like bell peppers and broccoli, and sometimes fruits like strawberries or oranges in small amounts.

👉 Make sure their diet includes Vitamin C regularly to keep them active and healthy.

3. Birds

Birds love a colorful and varied diet. While seeds and pellets are their main food, they also enjoy fresh fruits like bananas and apples, and veggies like carrots. You can give them nuts occasionally, but only in small amounts. And remember—some foods like chocolate, caffeine, and avocado are harmful to birds.

👉 Keep their food and water bowls clean every day to keep them safe from infections.

4. Fish

Feeding fish might seem simple, but it’s easy to overdo it. A small pinch of fish flakes or pellets once or twice a day is usually enough. Some fish also enjoy special treats like live or frozen food. But too much food can dirty the water and harm your fish.

👉 If there’s leftover food floating around, it’s best to remove it.

💧 A Few Simple Tips for All Small Pets

  • Always provide fresh, clean water
  • Stick to a regular feeding routine
  • Don’t overfeed
  • Introduce new foods slowly
  • Keep their eating area clean
