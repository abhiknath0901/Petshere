Grievance Policy

PetSphere – We Value Your Concerns

1. Introduction

PetSphere is committed to providing a transparent, efficient, and fair process for addressing user grievances. This policy outlines how complaints are registered, reviewed, and resolved.

2. What Issues Can Be Reported

  • Service-related concerns (grooming, adoption, rescue, or support)
  • Website or technical issues
  • Incorrect or misleading information
  • Behavior or conduct concerns during service delivery

3. How to Submit a Grievance

You may submit a grievance through any of the following channels:

  • Email: support@petsphere.com
  • Contact Form on the website
  • Customer Support Number (if applicable)

4. Grievance Handling Process

  • All grievances will be acknowledged within 48 hours of receipt.
  • A dedicated team member will review the issue and investigate if needed.
  • Resolution or an update will be provided within 7–10 business days.

5. Escalation Matrix

If you are not satisfied with the initial resolution, you may escalate the issue:

  • Level 1: Customer Support Team
  • Level 2: Grievance Redressal Officer
  • Level 3: Management Team

6. Confidentiality

All grievances and user information are handled with strict confidentiality. Personal details will only be shared when required for resolution.

7. Our Commitment

PetSphere strives to continuously improve its services and values every user’s feedback. Your concerns help us provide a better experience for you and your pets.

8. Contact

For grievance-related queries, please write to: grievance@petsphere.com
