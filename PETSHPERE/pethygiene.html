Pet Hygiene Essentials

Published February 2026 | 5 min read

Taking care of your small pet’s hygiene is more than just keeping them looking cute—it’s an important part of keeping them healthy and comfortable. Clean pets are happier, smell better, and are less likely to get infections or diseases. The good news? Maintaining hygiene for small pets is simple once you get into a routine.

1. Grooming Your Small Pets

  • Guineapigs and Hamster

    • These little ones usually clean themselves, but they may still need help sometimes. You can gently brush their fur to remove dirt and loose hair. Avoid bathing them with water, as it can make them sick—use dry baths or pet-safe powders if needed.

  • Rabbits

    • Rabbits are very clean animals, but brushing them regularly helps prevent fur tangles and hairballs. During shedding season, they may need more frequent grooming.

  • Birds

    • Birds love to clean themselves! You can provide a small bowl of water or lightly mist them so they can bathe on their own.

2. Cleaning Their Living Space

  • Remove leftover food daily
  • Change bedding regularly (at least once or twice a week)
  • Clean cages, tanks, or enclosures with pet-safe disinfectants
  • Make sure everything is dry before placing your pet back inside

3. Dental and Nail Care

Hygiene isn’t just about fur and cages—it also includes nails and teeth.

  • Nails: Trim them carefully when they grow too long
  • Teeth: Provide chew toys or hay to keep teeth from overgrowing

4. Water and Food Cleaniness

  • Wash food bowls and water bottles daily
  • Always provide fresh water
  • Avoid leaving old or spoiled food in the cage

❤️ A Few Simple Hygiene Tips

  • Keep a regular cleaning schedule
  • Handle your pet with clean hands
  • Watch for signs of illness like dull fur or unusual smell
  • Give your pet a stress-free, clean environment
